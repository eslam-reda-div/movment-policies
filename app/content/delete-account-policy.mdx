# Delete Account Policy

You can request to delete your account and associated personal data directly from within the Movement app.

## How to delete your account:

1. Open the Movement app
2. Go to your Profile page
3. Tap the "Delete Account" button
4. Confirm your choice to permanently delete your account

## Data that will be deleted:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Account credentials
- Trip history and reservations

Once deletion is completed, your account will no longer be accessible, and your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems.
